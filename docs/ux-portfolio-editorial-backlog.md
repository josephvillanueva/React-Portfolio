## Purpose

Move the portfolio beyond screenshots, technology lists, and demos. Show the problem being solved, who uses the experience, the key task flow, interaction and product trade-offs, what was implemented, and what remains to validate.

The site now features four deeper, explicitly evidence-aware project walkthroughs: Bayad, Kain @everyone, Spend Master, and Fraud Master. Other builds remain in the existing gallery.

## Evidence to gather before publishing stronger claims

For each flagship project, fill in the following using **real** artifacts:

1. **Personal contribution:** role, collaborators, project dates, and boundaries of work.
2. **Discovery evidence:** original problem statement, stakeholder input, user feedback, user/task research (if conducted).
3. **Early iterations:** sketches, wireframes, low-fidelity screens, alternatives that were considered, and feedback-driven changes.
4. **Design system evidence:** reusable components, accessibility considerations, responsive screens, interaction states.
5. **Validation:** usability tasks, methods, participant count, usability problems identified, and changes made (only if those studies actually happened).
6. **Outcomes:** verified before/after comparisons, adoption, task completion, error reduction, or qualitative feedback (only when data exists).
7. **Reflection:** trade-offs, limitations, what would be changed next.
8. **Permissions:** ensure internal work, company information, and other people's data are safe to publish.

## Editorial principles

- Prioritize three to five detailed studies over a long gallery of superficial cards.
- Let each case study stand alone: problem → audience → decisions → flow → result → reflection.
- Clearly separate implemented functionality from inferred design rationale and future testing plans.
- Never fabricate personas, interview quotes, testing, research results, team responsibilities, or numerical impact.
- Make summaries skimmable and details expandable; preserve keyboard access, text contrast, and motion preferences.
- When better evidence becomes available, update the narratives in `src/components/caseStudies/UXCaseStudies.jsx` rather than adding unverified marketing copy.
